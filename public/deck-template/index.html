Computational Taxonomy

The Three Pillars of In Silico Discovery

Virtual Screening vs. Physics Simulation vs. De Novo Design (MIT Lecture)

1. Physics Simulation (MD / GROMACS)

Rigorous atomistic dynamics, solvent thermodynamics, and RMSD stability for verified lead refinement.

2. Deep Virtual Screening (GNNs + Docking)

Ultra-fast scoring across 100,000+ candidates in minutes to filter high-affinity binding poses.

3. De Novo Generative AI

Deep generative models synthesize entirely new chemical structures optimized for target pocket geometry.

⭐ In this course, you master all 3 methods seamlessly in Google Colab.

Critical Perspective

AI & Drug Discovery — Two Vital Questions

Can AI replace computational chemists?
No — it amplifies you
AI accelerates molecular discovery:
  • Generates novel molecular scaffolds in seconds
  • Predicts 3D structures with AlphaFold3
  • Screens millions of chemical descriptors
  • Forecasts ADMET & toxicity profiles
  • Automates Python data pipelines
Why must you learn docking & dynamics?
Because AI hallucinates structures
Without physical validation, AI fails silently:
  • Generates molecules that are impossible to synthesize
  • Ignores steric clashes in binding active sites
  • Fails to account for solvent thermodynamics
  • Needs GROMACS & Vina to verify true physical binding
AI generates candidates — physical computation and your judgment validate them.
